Windows microphone transcription now rediscovers its installed portable runtime when the generated binding is missing or stale. Hands-free sends each completed utterance, shows Nice's normal text answer, speaks the final reply through bundled offline English Piper, then resumes listening. Stop, conversation changes and logout cancel stale voice work. Manual Mic remains draft-only.

Restart PortalAI normally after updating an open application. In Chat Preferences > Microphone & hands-free, Speak Nice's replies is enabled by default. This is turn-taking; voice barge-in and wake words are not implemented.
