# Windows r63: optional account privacy

Choose encryption and password requirements independently for each account, including administrators. Fresh installations can also open an account-free, unencrypted shared workspace. Existing profiles retain their current settings until you explicitly change them in **Accounts → Privacy and sign-in**.

Conversion closes the app through verified writer shutdown, rotates profile and registry master keys, preserves current data and deduplicated historical versions, converts account connections, and verifies replacement files and registry recovery before cleanup. Interrupted conversions have authenticated finish/rollback recovery. Plaintext profiles keep readable files and incremental recovery copies after logout. A password on plaintext storage locks the interface only; passwordless encryption keeps its key on the SSD. A passwordless administrator exposes its administrator access to other accounts. External backups retain their original protection.
