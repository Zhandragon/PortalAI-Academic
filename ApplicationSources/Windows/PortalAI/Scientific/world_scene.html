Objects and forces
Springs, ropes, pulleys and welds

Connect the selected object to another. Ropes model string/wire tension. Pulley supports sit 3 m above each endpoint; edit JSON for custom anchors and ratios.

Heat, fire and fracture

Lumped heat capacity, finite fuel energy. Ignite adds recorded ignition energy. Box breakage uses an impact-speed threshold and eight pre-cut fragments.

Convection, thermal links and external heaters are editable in the setup JSON.

Liquid and visual particles

“Liquid particles” runs physical SPH particles with pressure and viscosity. “Heat and buoyancy” uses a bulk liquid zone. Smoke, sparks and flames are lightweight visual particles; they do not exert forces.

Edit liquid dimensions, density, flow, temperature and particle counts in the setup JSON. Physical limits: 256 fluid particles and 128 rope nodes.

Explosion / radial impulse

A generic impulse burst at the position above, with sparks. No explosive materials, chemistry or blast-damage calculation. Bursts are manual and never replayed on load.

Setup JSON and files

Export keeps rigid positions, heat and fuel. Rope/fluid internal motion restarts. Applying JSON resets time. Inspect schema and examples through Nice’s physics.world_schema operation.
