PORTALAI PHYSICS LAB — 2026-10-16

OPEN AND USE
Ask Nice: "Call physics.world_schema, then make an interactive physics.world
scene with a spring, a hanging rope and a two-mass pulley. Include a comparison
with the ideal analytical prediction and state the assumptions."
Open world.html in the returned account outputs. It is self-contained and
offline. A WebGL-capable browser is required; no local server, Python, Node,
CDN or new download is needed. Documentation/Physics_Lab_Demo.html is a public
synthetic starter with all four presets. Nice can supply a complete bounded
JSON scene, not arbitrary executable game code.

Use Pause/Run, One step and Reset setup. Drag the view to orbit, wheel to zoom.
Load example replaces the setup. Select objects, add/move/remove them, apply
impulses or wind, and connect a second selected endpoint. The mechanism form
creates pulley anchors 3 m above the endpoints; use JSON for arbitrary fixed
supports and ratios. Heat/fuel and impact-break thresholds are editable.
Particle controls create visual emitters. The liquid-particle and buoyancy
presets expose both fluid approaches. All settings are available in the JSON
editor. Read current setup, edit, then Apply JSON explicitly resets simulation.

Save setup exports current rigid positions, velocities, temperatures, fuel and
active links. This is not an exact simulation checkpoint: internal rope/fluid
motion and visual particles restart, released loose rope nodes are omitted,
and radial bursts never replay. Constraint lengths tolerate up to 1% (minimum
1 cm) of solver stretch when importing/exporting; large inconsistencies fail.
Reset restores the last explicitly loaded/applied setup. It discards unsaved
edits. Simulations pause while the browser document is hidden.

PortalAI Stop cancels scene creation/publication using existing account/Stop
gates. It cannot stop an independently opened browser file: pause or close
that browser tab. No background service or GPU model is launched by the lab.

MODELS AND INPUTS
Units: metres, kilograms, seconds, Celsius; Y up and ground Y=0. The full input
schema is Scientific/world_schema.json, shared by Python and the browser.
physics.world_schema returns that schema, examples, limits and limitations.

objects: boxes/spheres, stable id, position/rotation/velocity/angular_velocity,
mass (0=fixed), size, color. Thermal fields: temperature, heat_capacity in J/K
(total body capacity, not specific heat), convection in W/K, fuel_j, burn_w,
ignition_c. break_speed is impact-normal speed in m/s, zero disables breakage.

Wind uses a uniform velocity and approximate projected area with quadratic
relative-air drag. The stable update approaches wind velocity without an
overshoot. This excludes lift, turbulent flow, shielding and rotating drag.

connections: kind spring/rope/pulley/weld, a and b body IDs. Springs use Hooke
force plus linear damping at body centers; unstable stiffness/damping versus
mass and fixed timestep is rejected. Ropes model wire/string tension with
massive segmented nodes and unilateral constraints; slack cannot push. There
is no bending stiffness, plasticity, knots or self-collision. Neighbor nodes
do not collide with their directly connected endpoint.

Ideal pulley equation: |A-anchor_a| + ratio*|B-anchor_b| <= length. Anchors are
fixed, massless, frictionless supports. Ratio changes mechanical advantage,
not wheel geometry/inertia. Welds lock relative position/orientation. Optional
break_force (N) and break_torque (N m; weld only) release overloaded links.
Released rope nodes retain their mass as free particles until explicit reset.
Solver tolerances and extreme mass ratios limit quantitative accuracy.

Pre-cut fracture divides a dynamic box into eight smaller boxes after the
declared impact-speed threshold. Mass, volume, heat capacity, fuel and initial
linear/angular velocity field are partitioned. Impact strength does not infer
material stress, cracks, fatigue, structural integrity or blast damage.

liquids: axis-aligned zones with position/size, density, flow, drag, temperature
and heat_transfer (W/K at full immersion). Archimedes buoyancy uses actual body
volume times its AABB overlap fraction, so rotated/partial immersion is an
approximation. Bulk liquid does not move or react back to bodies; use downward
Y gravity. No waves, liquid pressure field or phase change is solved.

fluids: actual Cannon SPH particles with position, counts [nx,ny,nz], spacing,
density, viscosity, sound_speed and color. Particle mass is density*spacing^3;
smoothing radius is 2*spacing. All clumps share one material/solver; material
parameters must match. Physical particles interact through pressure/viscosity
and collide with rigid bodies/ground. This is a small weakly-compressible demo,
not calibrated water, a general CFD solver or proof of fluid/material behavior.
Bulk zones and initial SPH regions cannot overlap. Speed/stability violations
pause rather than silently changing timestep or requested parameters.

Thermodynamics uses lumped body temperatures and capacities. Convection is
exact exponential relaxation to an ambient reservoir. Connection conductance
(W/K) uses energy-conserving pairwise exchange. Heaters distribute specified
power within a radius. These models omit internal temperature gradients,
latent heat, radiation, phase changes and full fluid heat transport.

Fire consumes finite fuel_j at burn_w when temperature reaches ignition_c;
half the energy heats the fuel body, half goes to nearby bodies (or is lost
if none are in range). Ignite explicitly supplies and records ignition energy.
There is no oxygen, reaction chemistry, material loss, smoke toxicity or fire
spread certification. Flame/smoke rendering is visual only.

Explosion control means a generic radial impulse event, with bounded scalar
impulse divided among nearby rigid bodies by distance. It emits sparks; it
does not estimate explosive yield, pressure waves, fragments or damage.

emitters: visual smoke/sparks/fire particles with position, velocity, rate and
lifetime. They move with simplified wind/gravity and exert no physical force.
Physical SPH particles and rope nodes are separate from these visual effects.

PERFORMANCE AND VALIDATION
Fixed physics step 1/120 second, at most 10 catch-up steps/frame, 80 constraint
iterations. Hard caps: 96 rigid bodies including reserved box fragments,
128 rope nodes, 48 connections per runtime, 256 physical fluid particles,
256 visual particles. Rendering resolution adapts; physical constants and
requested particle counts do not. Overload slows simulated time. Clearing
released rope nodes requires an explicit reset. High-speed discrete collisions
can tunnel. Nonfinite/out-of-bounds states stop with an error.

Automated numerical fixtures check spring period, pulley acceleration and
ratio, rope tension/slack, heat conservation/cooling, buoyancy, finite fuel,
impulse budget, fracture conservation, bounded particles and example runs.
Python checks schemas, routing, script escaping and existing science/account
publication contracts. Headless UI handler checks do not establish visual
browser acceptance or engineering fidelity. No physical GPU/recipient test
or CFD/combustion validation is implied.
