<h1 align="center">PortalAI Academic</h1>
<p align="center"><strong>Local AI for guided learning and classroom exploration.</strong></p>

Academic uses standard-only chat, vision and media. Full-only model assets and Portable Abliterator are excluded. It has no Full trial timer. [Edition details](Documentation/README.txt) · [Full edition](https://payhip.com/b/Kw54p) · [Try Full](https://payhip.com/b/ZeB0k).

## Make it your workspace

- **Chat and code:** work with Nice, documents and supported local tools.
- **Explore and create:** science, images, video, voice and music in one workspace.
- **Choose your models:** Standard-only models for learning, science and creative exploration; no trial timer.
- **Keep your work with you:** local accounts, saved projects and a portable drive layout.

1. **Extract the complete ZIP** into a new folder on a suitable drive.
2. **Open Start Setup:** `.cmd` on Windows, `.command` on Mac, or `bash prepare-setup.sh` on Linux.
3. **Choose your installation location and components.** Express starts small; models and companion utilities remain your choice.

**Already installed?** Exit PortalAI normally and choose **Update existing** on Windows. [Upgrade guide](Documentation/INSTALLATION.md#updating-an-existing-installation).

**Platform status:** Windows x64 r86 is the primary supported version. Native Mac/Linux r75 remain experimental, with limited feature parity. Universal contains a separate installer for each OS. Windows installers are unsigned. [Compatibility and drive formats](Documentation/INSTALLATION.md).

## Optional companions

Projection and Home Assistant setup are included as optional companions. Academic excludes Full-only model assets and Portable Abliterator. Configure connections and pair devices only when you choose to use them. [Companion setup](Documentation/PROJECTION.md).

## Your work, on your host

Supported local workflows can run offline after installation; web tools and remote integrations use the network. Password-protected accounts encrypt stored vaults and seal them on normal **Exit PortalAI**. Guest is shared, and data remains accessible while an account is unlocked. [Accounts and privacy](Documentation/INSTALLATION.md#accounts-and-support).
